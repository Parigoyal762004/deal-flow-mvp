## Today's Market Context (May 2026)

May 2026 was a tale of two markets in India: a handful of mega-rounds (Rapido $240M, Milky Mist ₹482Cr pre-IPO, Country Delight ₹200Cr debt) dominated the headline numbers, while the rest of the market was sharply selective with median deals under ₹50Cr. The clearest sectoral signals were: (1) pre-IPO dairy and FMCG platforms attracting Temasek-grade institutional capital, (2) defence and aerospace manufacturing seeing PE inflows following PLI scheme momentum, (3) profitable bootstrapped consumer brands (Yes Madam, Anveshan) finally taking institutional money after years of self-funding. For Akro, the standout outreach window is with companies that just closed their first institutional round (Yes Madam) or are in early pre-IPO capital-raising mode (Milky Mist, Country Delight, Rapido) — these founders are actively building their institutional investor relationships right now, making Akro's timing excellent.
