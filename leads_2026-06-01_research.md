## Today's Market Context

India's startup ecosystem in Q1–Q2 2026 is undergoing a clear bifurcation: early-stage funding surged 58% YoY while late-stage (₹100M+) deals dried up for the first time since 2022. This means growth-stage companies at Series B–D are navigating a tighter capital environment, making Akro's investor-connect proposition highly timely. The sectors seeing the most funding activity are home services (Snabbit, Pronto, Yes Madam), fintech (Scapia, Solfin, Sahi), EVs (Euler Motors, Zeno, Battery Smart), and manufacturing/hard tech (Iscon Balaji, Agrow Allied, SFO Technologies, Kimbal). Macro tailwinds are strong: PLI scheme disbursements are accelerating in electronics, food processing, and chemicals; RBI held rates steady through Q1 2026 making debt more accessible; and India's export markets are opening further under newly signed FTAs with the UAE and ASEAN bloc. For Akro, this translates to the best outreach timing in two years — founders at Series B/C are actively seeking institutional relationships as they scale beyond their existing investor networks.
