## Why This Batch Is Different

Previous batches targeted companies that had **already raised**. This batch identifies companies that are **about to raise** — showing signals like DRHP filing, SEBI approval, announced fundraise plans, or strong growth with no recent capital. Akro's highest-value play is arriving before the banker pitch deck.

**Signal Categories Used:**
- ✅ SEBI IPO Approval received — listing imminent, pre-IPO placement window open
- 📄 DRHP filed — 60–120 day window before potential IPO or pre-IPO round
- 📣 Publicly announced upcoming raise (amount & purpose disclosed)
- 📈 Revenue milestone + ESOP/buyback signal — institutional raise imminent
