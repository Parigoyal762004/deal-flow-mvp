## Market Context

The ₹50–100Cr D2C bracket is Akro's most compelling outreach window in 2026. These founders have proven product-market fit, shown multi-year revenue consistency, and are now hitting the "first real institutional round" wall — exactly where Akro's investor-connect and advisory value is highest. Four clear trends in this bracket: (1) FemTech / women's wellness brands (Sirona, Pee Safe, Plush) crossing ₹50–80Cr with growing institutional backing; (2) Pet care (Vetic, Supertails) seeing 2-3x growth as India's pet economy explodes; (3) D2C accessories and lifestyle brands (uppercase, DailyObjects, SleepyCat) crossing ₹80–110Cr with Series B rounds; (4) Niche content and tech platforms (Eloelo, Stage) showing explosive GMV growth. Across all four, the outreach timing is now — most are either in or approaching their Series B/C cycle.
